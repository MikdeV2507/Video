## Content brief

**Learning objective:** after this episode the viewer can explain why racks are placed in a
hot aisle / cold aisle arrangement, and can check a row layout for correct orientation,
grid alignment and the common mistakes that mix hot and cold air.

**Three key points**
1. All racks in a row face the same way; rows face each other front-to-front and back-to-back.
2. Align rows to the floor grid so the cold aisle gets full perforated tiles.
3. Keep hot and cold air apart: no racks turned the wrong way, no open gaps, no perforated tiles in the hot aisle.

**Don't / Do:** a rack turned the wrong way, an open gap in a row, perforated tiles in the hot aisle — versus the correct hot/cold aisle layout.

**Standards referenced:** TIA-942, EN 50600-2-3 (environmental control), ASHRAE TC9.9.

**Illustrative values:** the row temperatures in scene 4 (22 / 26 / 30 / 34 °C) are exaggerated for clarity, not measured values.

**Pronunciation:** CRAH = "cray"; ASHRAE = "ash-ray".
